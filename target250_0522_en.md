## 1 Building labels and how to read the guide

### Building labels

F means Car factory; R means Car dealership. F0 and R0 are the existing buildings; build F1–F7 on empty plots and keep their labels throughout the run. L means building level; Q0 means quality 0. Do not buy research.

After scrapping the four starting buildings, use the 7 empty plots for F1–F7. You will have 8 Car factories and 1 Car dealership.

### Notation and timing

- P001 and similar labels identify production orders; P0 is the starting Power plant. Sell back means selling instantly from the Warehouse; sell trucks through the Car dealership.
- All times are elapsed challenge time (hours:minutes:seconds) at 2× speed. Do not halve them again. Amounts are in-game currency; selling prices are per truck.
- The opening checklist is pause 001; continue with pause 002 afterward. Use the construction overview for a quick reference and the full schedule to execute the plan.

## 2 Opening setup and full schedule

### Opening: complete within a single pause

Confirm you are in the Challenge Realm and start from a full reset: cash \$1,350,000, 0 trucks, 75 Sim Boosts and 9 plots. Starting Warehouse: 460 Steel; 10 each of On-board computer, Basic interior and Car body; 300 each of Chemicals and Electronic components; 20,000 Power; 5,000 Cotton; 200 Crude oil. ### Execution rules

- Pausing: wait at least 1 second after an event is ready before pausing to act. Buildings handled together share this wait; other buildings keep running during it. Each row is one pause; resume afterward. Initial setup before the timer starts is exempt. Collecting cash or goods does not count as an operation.
- Cash receipts: R0 sells at \$3,855 per truck throughout, following the 9 listed sale orders totaling 39 trucks. Use only the cash automatically credited when a whole sale order finishes; no partial cash pickups are needed. Automatic receipts appear in the next operation's check column.
- Truck pickups: during production, collect in batches of about 3 trucks, then collect any remainder when the order finishes. Wait 1 second after pickup becomes available. Collect all stock needed before placing a sale order. Listed pickup quantities are planning references; follow actual in-game availability.
- Inputs and payment: each truck requires 10 Steel, 1 On-board computer, 1 Basic interior, 1 Car body and 6 Combustion engines. Combine purchases of the same resource within each pause and subtract existing stock. Prepay each full order's wages and keep at least \$500 afterward. F0's first three orders use the inputs for 95 trucks bought at the start.
- Order of actions: collect trucks and confirm cash receipts, then handle ready downgrades or scrapping and sell back the recovered materials. Next buy inputs together, start production and finally start new sales. Process factories in the order F0→F1→F2→F6→F4→F5→F7→F3; place orders only in the buildings listed for that pause.
- Downgrading and scrapping: use each factory's own completed output. Downgrade F0 after 95 trucks and scrap it after 99; scrap F1 after 16 and F2 after 20; scrap R0 after its 9th sale order. Finish the whole order, collect its output and wait at least 1 second. Use recovered materials to fund the next batch only after the Sell back cash arrives.
- Cash shortages: full input and wage payments must leave at least \$500; otherwise wait for actual income or proceeds from the planned downgrades and scrapping. Keep the quantities and sequence of all 40 production orders and 9 sale orders; do not shrink batches or add orders. Retain F3–F7 after each produces 23 trucks.
