### Execution rules

- Pausing: wait at least 1 second after an event is ready before pausing to act. Buildings handled together share this wait; other buildings keep running during it. Each row is one pause; resume afterward. Initial setup before the timer starts is exempt. Collecting cash or goods does not count as an operation.
- Cash receipts: each sale order settles automatically when it finishes; no partial cash pickups are needed. Unreceived income cannot fund orders. If a receipt makes an order affordable, wait at least 1 second after the cash arrives before submitting it.
- Truck pickups: during production, collect in batches of about 3 trucks, then collect any remainder when the order finishes. Wait 1 second after pickup becomes available. Collect all stock needed before placing a sale order. If Warehouse stock is short, check for uncollected factory output. Total production includes trucks already sold and completed trucks awaiting pickup.
- Inputs: each truck requires 10 Steel, 1 On-board computer, 1 Basic interior, 1 Car body and 6 Combustion engines, all Q0. When a row says to buy inputs for N trucks together, multiply this recipe by N, subtract existing stock and combine purchases of the same resource. Prepay wages for each full production order.
- Order of actions: first handle ready downgrades or scrapping and receive the Sell back proceeds; then buy inputs together, start production and finally start new sales. Process factories in the order F0→F1→F2 and dealerships in the order R0→R6. Skip any full production order you cannot yet afford and handle other ready actions; do not shrink production batches.
- Downgrading and scrapping: factories use their own completed output, as shown above. Dealerships use company-wide completed output. Their thresholds to downgrade one level / stop new sales and scrap after the current sale are R0 675/702, R1 639/656, R2 719/729, R3 670/686, R4 702/711, R5 721/725 and R6 611/619. Finish the current order, collect any finished trucks and wait 1 second before acting. If both conditions are met, scrap directly. Downgrade each dealership only once: R0–R5 go from L3→L2 and R6 from L2→L1. Reserve 12 sets recovered from F1 for R6 and sell only the remaining 15 sets.
- Cash shortages: keep the last dealership until you can fund all remaining full production orders as cash arrives. If more cash is needed, sell extra trucks one at a time at the current price. While production orders remain unplaced, F0 or F2 may be scrapped for funds once its full quota is complete, its output is collected and 1 second has passed. Keep factories with unfinished quotas; the reference plan retains F0 at L1 and F2 at L2.
