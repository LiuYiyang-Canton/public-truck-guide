## 1 Building labels and how to read the guide

### Notation and timing

- P001 and similar labels identify production orders; P0 is the starting Power plant. Sell back means selling instantly from the Warehouse; sell trucks through Car dealerships.
- All times are elapsed challenge time (hours:minutes:seconds) at 2× speed. Do not halve them again. Amounts are in-game currency; selling prices are per truck.
- The opening checklist is pause 001; continue with pause 002 afterward. Use the construction overview for a quick reference and the full schedule to execute the plan.

## 2 Opening setup and full schedule

### Opening: complete within a single pause

### Execution rules

- Pausing: wait at least 1 second after an event is ready before pausing to act. Buildings handled together share this wait; other buildings keep running during it. Each row is one pause; resume afterward. Initial setup before the timer starts is exempt. Collecting cash or goods does not count as an operation, but you must still collect them.
- Cash pickups: every 61 seconds, collect from all eligible dealerships, at 03:12:09, 03:13:10, 03:14:11 and so on. This schedule is measured from challenge time zero. A new sale order's first partial pickup must be at least 12 minutes 01 seconds after it starts; later pickups must be at least 61 seconds apart. Short orders settle automatically on completion. Collect only when Pickup Cash is available.
- Truck pickups: during production, collect in batches of about 3 trucks, then collect any remainder when the order finishes. Wait 1 second after pickup becomes available. Collect all stock needed before placing a sale order; if Warehouse stock is short, check for uncollected factory output. If a cash pickup makes a new order affordable, wait another 1 second after receiving the cash before submitting it.
- Inputs: each truck requires 10 Steel, 1 On-board computer, 1 Basic interior, 1 Car body and 6 Combustion engines. When a row says to buy inputs for N trucks together, multiply this recipe by N, subtract existing stock and combine purchases of the same resource. Prepay wages for each full production order.
- Order of actions: first downgrade or scrap buildings and receive the Sell back proceeds; then buy inputs together, start production and finally start new sales. Process factories in the order F0→F2→F1 and dealerships in the order R0→R5. Skip any full order you cannot yet afford and handle other ready actions; do not shrink batches, cancel ongoing sales or change their prices.
- Downgrading and scrapping: factories use their own completed output, as shown above. Dealerships use company-wide completed output. Their thresholds to downgrade one level / stop new sales and scrap after the current sale are R0 462/467, R1 439/443, R2 442/479, R3 452/453, R4 443/450 and R5 463/472. Finish the current order, collect any finished trucks and wait 1 second before acting. If both downgrade and scrap conditions are met, scrap directly.
- Cash shortages: keep the last dealership until you can fund all remaining full production orders as cash arrives. If planned sale orders run out and you still need cash, sell extra trucks one at a time at that dealership's last listed price. While production orders remain unplaced, a factory that has finished its entire quota may be scrapped for funds after collecting its output and waiting 1 second. Keep factories with unfinished quotas.
